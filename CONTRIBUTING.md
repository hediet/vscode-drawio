# Contributing

Contributions are welcome as issues and as pull requests.

A contribution needs no signed agreement and no sign-off line.
A contribution comes under the license of this repository, the GNU General Public License, version 3, in `LICENSE.md`.
A contribution to a covered file of `LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md` also comes under the additional permission there, so that the output of the extension stays free of the license.
Section D.6 of the terms of service of GitHub licenses a contribution under the license that the repository gives notice of.
The contributor keeps the copyright of the contribution.

By opening a pull request, you confirm that you have the right to give the contribution under these terms.

The project spells "color" (American spelling) in its names, comments, messages and documents.
